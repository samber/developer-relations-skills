Mostly right, but the "send to all five at once" part is the weak link. Manager, staff engineers, and PMM will likely want structural/content changes — legal and the CEO are your scarcest reviewers, and you don't want them marking up a version that's still moving under peer feedback (means re-review later, wasted senior time). Sequence it: prose polish → peer pass (manager, staff engineers, PMM) → fold in changes → then legal + CEO on a near-final version. Collecting everyone's comments live in one doc also risks duplicate/conflicting feedback if reviewers can't see each other's threads — worth deciding upfront whether comments get resolved as you go or batched for a single pass.
