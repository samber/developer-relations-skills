## How to spend your 120 min/week

**Tier 1 — watch everything, non-negotiable (~27 min/wk):** Apollo GraphQL, Hasura, The Guild, WunderGraph. Small channels, cheap in aggregate, and this is literally your competitive set — direct product and content intelligence on the category you work in.

**Tier 2 — pick 1–2 highlight videos a week, skip the rest (~60–70 min/wk budget, not full cadence):** Cloudflare and Postman first (closest DevRel-craft peers to your job), then rotate in Google for Developers or Microsoft Developer when a title looks genuinely relevant (new API-design pattern, a talk on developer-experience metrics, etc.) rather than every upload.

**Tier 3 — ignore weekly, sweep monthly instead:** GitHub, MongoDB, JetBrainsTV, Docker, HashiCorp, DigitalOcean, Vercel, Kong, AWS. AWS especially — its raw volume (~90 min/wk if followed fully) will eat your whole budget for the lowest signal-per-minute of anyone on this list; treat it as a search target ("AWS re:Invent API gateway talk") rather than a subscription.

That's ~95–100 min/week fully allocated, leaving slack for whatever a Tier 3 sweep turns up.
